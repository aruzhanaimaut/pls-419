# Із: making sure a call for help leaves a trace

PLS 419 final project. A public website on the police response to domestic violence in Kazakhstan, the data behind it, current research, and a proposed AI tool (Із, "trace" in Kazakh) with its environmental, economic, labour, governance and data-ethics costs.

**Live site:** https://aruzhanaimaut.github.io/pls-419/

## Structure

- `index.html`: the four sections (Problem, Background and data, Research, AI solution with the bill and verdict), the AI transparency statement and sources
- `style.css`: layout and design
- `app.js`: charts (Figures 1–4), research tabs, the legal-measures panel, the bill calculator and the verdict switch
- `fonts/`: Literata and Golos, subset to Latin and Cyrillic, including Kazakh letters

## Data used in the figures

- Figure 1: 2017 Sample Survey on Violence Against Women in Kazakhstan, Table 6.4 (PDF p. 80)
- Figure 2: Human Rights Commissioner of Kazakhstan, 2025 statement on family and domestic violence
- Figure 3: Kazakhstan MICS 2024 Survey Findings Report, Table PR.7.1W (PDF p. 230)

Every statistic on the site links to its source in the Sources section.
